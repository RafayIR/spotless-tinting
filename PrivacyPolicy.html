This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Who this Policy covers

This Policy applies to:

  • Customers who book pickups, deliveries, or rides
  • Drivers who use the driver App to receive and complete jobs
  • Company users who place or manage delivery orders for a business

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account details: first name, last name, mobile number, email address (if provided), city, language preference, and profile photo
  • Authentication: one-time passwords (OTPs) sent by SMS to verify that you control the phone number
  • Order details: pickup and drop-off addresses and coordinates, recipient name and contact details, goods or trip information, and any notes you add
  • Driver identity and KYC: CNIC number and expiry date, CNIC front and back images, driving licence number, type, expiry date, and licence images
  • Vehicle details: vehicle type, plate number, name, colour, year of make, and vehicle photos
  • Wallet and deposits: wallet balance, deposit amounts, and images of deposit slips submitted for approval
  • Support: messages, complaints, or queries you send to us

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Precise location: GPS latitude and longitude while you use features that need location (for example, setting pickup/drop-off, matching Drivers to nearby Orders, and showing a Driver’s position during a trip)
  • Background location (Drivers): if you go online or are on an active job, we may collect location while the App is in the background so we can offer nearby Orders, apply booking radius and zone matching, and let the Customer/Company track fulfilment
  • Device information: device identifiers used for login sessions and push notifications (including Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens), platform (Android/iOS), and similar technical data
  • Usage and logs: App activity such as login times, order status changes, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and secure the Services

2.3 Information from others

  • A Company may provide recipient and delivery details when it creates an Order
  • A Customer’s location and Order details may be shared with an assigned Driver so the job can be completed
  • A Driver’s name, photo, vehicle, plate number, and live location may be shown to the Customer/Company during an Order

We do not knowingly collect information from children. The App is intended for users 18 years of age or older.

3. Why we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify your mobile number
  • Match Drivers to Orders by zone, vehicle type, and distance from pickup
  • Show maps, routes, and live tracking during an Order
  • Verify Drivers (KYC and vehicle documents) before they can accept jobs
  • Process wallet deductions and review Driver deposits
  • Send OTPs, transactional messages, and push notifications (job offers, status updates)
  • Provide customer support and investigate disputes or safety issues
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests
  • Improve reliability, safety, and features of the App

Legal bases (where applicable): performance of a contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating a safe logistics service, compliance with legal obligations, and consent (for example, location or notification permissions on your device).

4. Location data (important for Google Play)

Location is core to the Services.

  • Customers: we use location to set pickup/drop-off points and to show nearby fulfilment.
  • Drivers: we use precise location, including while the App is in the background when you are online or on a job, to offer Orders, enforce service zones and booking radius, and share your position with the Customer/Company until the job ends.

You can turn location off in your device settings. If you do, many features (especially Driver matching and live tracking) will not work.

We do not sell location data. We do not use location for unrelated advertising.

5. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Services:

  • Other users on a job: Customers/Companies and Drivers see the information required to complete that Order (contacts, addresses, vehicle, live location during the trip)
  • Service providers: SMS OTP delivery, cloud hosting and databases, file/media storage for photos and documents, maps, email, and push notifications (for example Firebase). These providers process data on our instructions
  • LOADe staff: Super Admins and operations staff who approve Drivers, Companies, deposits, and handle support
  • Legal and safety: if required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, or property

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Permissions the App may request

Depending on the App (Customer or Driver) and your device:

  • Location (precise; background for Drivers when online/on a job)
  • Camera and/or photos/media (profile, CNIC, licence, vehicle, deposit slips)
  • Notifications
  • Phone/SMS-related access only as required by the OS to receive or auto-read OTPs, if you grant it

You can refuse a permission; related features may then be unavailable.

7. How long we keep information

We keep account, Order, KYC, vehicle, wallet, and location history for as long as your account is active and as needed for operations, safety, accounting, dispute handling, and legal retention. When we no longer need the data, we delete or anonymise it, unless a longer period is required by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures (including authenticated APIs and access controls) to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please keep your device and OTP codes confidential.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law (including the laws of Pakistan), you may:

  • Access or update profile details in the App
  • Request a copy of personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data, except where we must retain it (for example completed Orders, financial records, or legal claims)
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions in device settings

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity (usually via your registered mobile number).

10. International processing

Some providers (for example cloud storage, maps, or push notifications) may process data on servers outside Pakistan. Where that happens, we take steps appropriate to the service so that your information remains protected.

11. Third-party links and SDKs

The App may use third-party SDKs (maps, crash/diagnostics, notifications). Their processing is governed by their own policies as well as this one. We are not responsible for websites or services we do not control.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change, and we may notify you in the App or by other reasonable means. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
